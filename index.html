Colposcopy

A colposcopy is a follow-up examination. A healthcare professional looks more closely at the cervix.

Why am I seeing this?

In this simulation, your healthcare provider has already selected this as your next step. CarePath is not making this medical decision.

Your action

Find a provider or facility where you can complete the colposcopy, then add the step to your care plan.

CarePath does not diagnose cancer or recommend medical treatment. It only organizes information already provided by a healthcare professional.
